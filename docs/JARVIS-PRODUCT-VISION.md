# Jarvis — Product Vision (2026-11-05)

Jarvis is an offline-first, AI-powered Android personal assistant and practical Kampala/Uganda life, travel, relocation and international-community concierge. **Geographic search is a supporting capability, not the whole product.**

Core capabilities:
- Natural multi-turn AI reasoning, voice recognition, text-to-speech, contextual memory, local/offline AI where supported, and permission-aware Android actions.
- Kampala city and newcomer guidance: transport, shopping, education, healthcare, banking, telecommunications, government services, landmarks, attractions, faith and cultural services.
- Embassies, high commissions, consulates, immigration, passports and official consular guidance.
- International community guidance for Indian, Chinese, Sudanese, Eritrean, European, Somali, Ethiopian, South Sudanese, Nigerian, Turkish and other communities: publicly advertised languages, cultural associations, restaurants, groceries, schools, worship, interpreters and events. Never infer ethnicity or private residence.
- Flights and airport services, Uber/ride providers, hotels/BnBs/apartments, restaurant discovery/reservations and order tracking, tourism and event itineraries.
- Complete supported activities from discovery through comparison, planning, user-approved provider handoff/booking, provider-confirmed results and tracking. Never invent availability, booking, payment, ride status, prices or live information.
- Source-backed nationwide discovery, GPS consent, context-aware destination follow-ups, user-controlled navigation and privacy/accessibility/reliability.

Implementation does not imply verification or production readiness. Proposed extensions (multimodal document support, wake word and broad autonomous planning) must not be silently inserted as completed or agreed milestones.

**Development rule:** Check this vision and the roadmap before proposing work. Mark proven milestones complete; do not reopen completed features or invent milestones. Local uncommitted code may be ahead of GitHub. Preserve prior work on AI, voice, memory, Android actions, offline inference and geography.
